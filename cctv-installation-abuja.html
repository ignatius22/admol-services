Security Systems

Useful camera coverage begins with a site survey

Admoltech plans, installs and configures CCTV systems for homes, offices, institutions and commercial properties in Abuja. We first identify the areas that need observation, important entry points, lighting conditions and how footage should be stored or accessed.

The installation can then be planned around camera placement, cabling, recording, network access and power availability.

  • Camera-position planning
  • Recording configuration
