An inverter system should start with a load assessment

Admoltech plans and installs inverter and battery systems for homes, offices, commercial premises and institutions in Abuja. Before equipment is selected, we identify the appliances and circuits that need backup, expected operating time and the available charging options.

This practical assessment helps align inverter capacity, battery storage, protection and wiring with the actual requirement instead of relying on guesswork.
