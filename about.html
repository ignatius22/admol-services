Who We Are

Admol Services is a premier energy solutions provider based in Abuja, Nigeria. We was founded on the belief that clean, reliable power should be accessible to every home and business, regardless of the challenges of the national grid.

  • OUR VISION
  • QUALITY
  • COMMUNITY

Our vision is to lead Nigeria's transition to a sustainable energy future. By providing high-efficiency solar installations, we help our clients break free from the noise, pollution, and rising costs of diesel generators.

We don't just sell panels; we build long-term energy security for our customers, ensuring that "the light stays on" even when the grid fails.

Partner With Us

At Admol Services, quality is non-negotiable. We source our inverters, batteries, and solar panels from globally recognized manufacturers to ensure every system survives the Nigerian climate and delivers peak performance for years.

Our engineering team follows strict international standards for every installation, from small residential setups to large-scale industrial arrays.

We believe in empowering the local community. Beyond installations, we offer training and consulting to help Nigerians understand and maintain their own energy systems.

Every project we complete contributes to a cleaner, greener Nigeria, reducing the national carbon footprint one roof at a time.
